Create injury.py module with engineer_injury_features(player_stats, injuries) function:

Injury severity encoding (research: 8-10% production drop for non-QB injuries):
- Out: 1.0 (100% impact - player won't play)
- Doubtful: 0.75 (75% impact - unlikely to play)
- Questionable: 0.5 (50% impact - may play limited)
- Probable: 0.25 (25% impact - likely to play, slight limitation) [deprecated 2016 but in historical data]
- Not on report: 0.0 (no impact)

Features created:
- injury_severity: float 0.0-1.0 based on report_status
- on_injury_report: binary 0/1 flag (1 if report_status is not null)

Implementation details:
- Join injuries to player_stats on [gsis_id, season, week]
- For multiple injury reports per player-week (updated during week), take most recent by date_modified
- Use .group_by(['gsis_id', 'season', 'week']).last() to get final status before game
- Handle missing injuries (left join, fill_null with 0.0/0)
