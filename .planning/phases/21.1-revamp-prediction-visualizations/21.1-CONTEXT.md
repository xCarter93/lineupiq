# Phase 21.1: Revamp Prediction Visualizations - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-01-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<vision>
## How This Should Work

This phase builds a **player lookup repository** where users can browse all NFL players across all positions (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DEF), click on any player, and see their historic performance overlaid with what our models would have predicted for the entire 2025 season.

The goal is to prove model quality by showing "here's what we would have predicted vs what actually happened" for every week of 2025. Users should be able to see at a glance how accurate our predictions were, both at a high level (aggregate +/- metrics across all predictions) and at an individual player level (week-by-week comparison charts).

This isn't just about visualization polish - it's about building the **credible foundation** for the prediction system by validating it against real 2025 data. When users see that our 2025 predictions were accurate, they'll trust our 2026+ predictions.

The player repository serves as the central hub: filter by position, click a player, see a drawer slide in from the right with tabs showing their stats, history, and 2025 predicted vs actual performance.

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

- **Proper time-series validation** - Do this RIGHT even if it means more work. Retrain models week-by-week through end of 2024, then predict each week of 2025 (Weeks 1-18) for all players/positions. No shortcuts.

- **Player repository for all 6 positions** - Comprehensive lookup covering QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DEF. Users can filter by position and find any player they want to validate.

- **High-level aggregate accuracy metrics** - Show overall +/- prediction vs actuals across ALL predictions. Users should immediately understand "how good are these models?" before diving into individual players.

- **Infrastructure that scales to future seasons** - Set this up so we can easily carry it forward into 2026, 2027, etc. The week-over-week prediction tracking should be sustainable, not a one-time analysis.

</essential>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

**Player Drawer Pattern:**
- Use shadcn Sheet component sliding in from right (mobile-friendly, modern)
- Tabbed organization inside: Overview (key stats), History (historic performance charts), 2025 Validation (predicted vs actual), Predictions (future weeks when available)

**2025 Validation Visualization:**
- Week-by-week line charts showing predicted value vs actual value across the 2025 season
- Both model performance dashboard (aggregate view) AND individual player sheets (player-specific view)
- Users can explore from macro (how good are the models overall?) to micro (how accurate for this specific player?)

**UI Foundation:**
- Player repository with position filters (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DEF)
- Click player → drawer opens with tabs
- This becomes the foundation for future phases (lineup builder, scoring settings, advanced visualizations)

</specifics>

<notes>
## Additional Context

**Multi-Phase Vision:**
This is Phase 21.1 of a larger vision to transform the app from a matchup prediction tool into a full fantasy lineup powerhouse. Future phases will add:
- Lineup builder with position slots
- Custom scoring settings
- Advanced Recharts visualizations (synchronized charts, multi-stat comparisons)
- Season-long projection views

**Phase 21.1 Scope:**
Focus purely on player repository + 2025 validation. Prove the models work. Build the player drawer UI pattern that future phases will extend.

**Emphasis on "Doing It Right":**
User is willing to invest in proper time-series validation (multiple training sessions, week-by-week predictions) rather than shortcuts. The goal is sustainable infrastructure that carries into 2026+ seasons, not a one-time proof-of-concept.

**Technical Approach:**
The validation system will likely require:
1. Retrain all 32 models using data through end of 2024
2. For each week of 2025 (Weeks 1-18):
   - Load player features for that week
   - Run predictions for all active players/positions
   - Store predictions in Convex
3. Compare stored predictions against actual 2025 stats (already available)
4. Calculate aggregate accuracy metrics (MAE, R², +/- by position/stat)
5. Display in dual views: aggregate dashboard + individual player charts

</notes>
