# Phase 18: Performance Fixes & Optimization - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-01-20
**Status:** Ready for planning

<vision>
## How This Should Work

The app should feel fast and polished across the board - from initial page load to every interaction. When someone uses LineupIQ, they should experience smooth, responsive interactions that feel intentional and well-crafted.

The key is comprehensive optimization: faster page loads, smoother transitions between states, and better data caching. It's not just about fixing one specific lag issue - it's about creating an overall performant experience where everything feels snappy and fluid.

When data needs to load, the experience should still feel smooth with proper loading states and transitions. No jarring blank screens or freezing - just polished, professional loading behavior.

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

This phase requires a holistic approach where three aspects work together:

- **Player selection responsiveness** - The dropdown/search interaction must feel instant and smooth
- **Smart data loading strategy** - Loading the right data at the right time to avoid slowdowns or waste
- **Polished loading experience** - When loading is necessary, it feels smooth and intentional, never janky or blocking

All three are equally important - they combine to create the overall performant experience. Neglecting any one would undermine the others.

</essential>

## Specific Ideas

No specific requirements - open to standard approaches and best practices for Next.js performance optimization.

## Additional Context
